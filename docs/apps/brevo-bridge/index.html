The problem

Your customers are in your orders, not in your campaigns

  • Every order carries a name, a phone number and an email, and none of it reaches your mailing tool.
  • Exporting customers by hand waits for a quiet day that never comes.
  • A newsletter goes to everyone, the regular of 5 years and the customer of 1 night.
  • Customers who stopped ordering are noticed months later, if at all.

What it does

Your customers in Brevo, already sorted

  • Saved with every new order

    Once the bridge is on, each new HubRise order and its customer are saved in your Brevo account. There is nothing to export.

  • Up to 10 customer groups

    Create a group for each audience you want to reach, such as new customers, regulars, or those who have not ordered in a while.

  • Rules set with sliders

    Sort customers by number of orders, total spent, loyalty points and the dates of their first and last orders, then by the service of their last order and by zip code.

  • Consent in the rules

    A group can also be sorted by marketing consent, for email and for SMS, as customers gave it when they ordered.

  • Only the orders you choose

    Every order is synced by default. You can keep only some services, such as takeout, or only some channels, such as your own.

  • Campaigns sent from Brevo

    Write and send your emails and SMS in Brevo. The bridge's menu links straight to Brevo's email, SMS, contacts and orders pages.

How it works

Connected in 4 steps

  1. Step 1

    Define your groups

    Name each group and set its rules.

  2. Step 2

    Choose the orders

    All of them, or only the services and channels you pick.

  3. Step 3

    Add your Brevo API key

    You create it in Brevo. If Brevo blocks unknown IP addresses, the setup shows you how to allow the bridge.

  4. Step 4

    Turn the bridge on

    From then on, each new order and its customer are saved in Brevo.

Questions

Questions about Brevo Bridge

Do I need a Brevo account?

Yes. The bridge saves customers and orders in your own Brevo account, connected with an API key you create in Brevo. Brevo has its own plans, billed by Brevo.

When does the bridge start saving customers?

As soon as you turn it on. From then on, each new order that reaches HubRise is saved in Brevo with its customer.

How are customers sorted into groups?

By the rules you set for each group: number of orders, total spent, loyalty points, dates of the first and last orders, service of the last order, zip code and marketing consent.

Who sends the campaigns?

You do, from Brevo. The bridge keeps your customers and groups up to date, and Brevo's own tools write and send the campaigns.

Do I need a HubRise account to use Brevo Bridge?

Yes. Brevo Bridge connects to your store's HubRise account, where your POS and your delivery platforms already send their orders. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
