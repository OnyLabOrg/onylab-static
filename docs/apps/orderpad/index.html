The problem

The phone rings in the middle of the rush

  • The order is written on a pad, then typed into the POS once the call is over.
  • The address and the phone number are asked for again, even for a regular.
  • Delivery fees and minimum orders are worked out from memory, call after call.
  • Nobody knows how many orders came by phone last week, or at what time.

What it does

Everything a phone order needs, on 1 screen

  • The caller, found from their number

    Type 5 characters of a phone number or an email, and OrderPad suggests the matching customers from your HubRise customer list. Picking one fills in the name, the contact details, the saved address and the marketing consents.

  • Delivery, takeout or eat-in

    Choose the service for each order. Delivery addresses complete as you type, eat-in orders take a table, and any order can be as soon as possible or set for a later date and time.

  • Your menu, 2 ways

    Browse the HubRise menu by category, with its options and a note per item, or type names and references on the keyboard in free entry. OrderPad remembers the way you prefer for each location.

  • Fees set once

    Set a delivery fee for each distance band, up to 50 km from your store, and a minimum order value for delivery. When a delivery order falls short, a minimum order fee makes up the difference.

  • Orders you can still change

    Until an order is completed, you can reopen it to change the time, the table, the items, the fees and the notes. The customer, the service and the delivery address stay as they were taken.

  • Your phone orders, in figures

    A dashboard shows orders, revenue and average order value for any period, compared with the period before, with orders by day and hour, by service and by channel.

How it works

From the call to the POS in 4 steps

  1. Step 1

    Find the caller

    Type the number. The customer's details and address come up from HubRise, or you add a new customer.

  2. Step 2

    Choose the service

    Delivery, takeout or eat-in, as soon as possible or at a set time.

  3. Step 3

    Add the items

    From the menu or on the keyboard, with options, quantities and notes. The totals, the fees and the VAT are worked out as you go.

  4. Step 4

    Send the order

    OrderPad creates the order in HubRise, which passes it to your POS. You find it in the day's order list, with its status.

Questions

Questions about OrderPad

Can OrderPad find a customer who has ordered before?

Yes. From 5 characters of a phone number or an email, it searches your HubRise customer list and fills in the details and the address of the customer you pick.

Can I take eat-in orders with OrderPad?

Yes. Choose eat-in and type a table, or pick one of the tables set up in OrderPad's settings.

Can I change an order after sending it?

Yes, until it is completed. You can change the time, the table, the items, the fees and the notes. The customer, the service and the delivery address cannot be changed.

How are delivery fees set?

You set a fee for each distance band, up to 50 km from your store, and a minimum order value for delivery. Staff can choose another of your fees on any order.

Do I need a HubRise account to use OrderPad?

Yes. OrderPad connects to your store's HubRise account, where your POS and your delivery platforms already send their orders. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
