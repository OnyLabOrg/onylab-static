OrderPicker · Kitchen display and picking screen

Prepare every order without a missing item

OrderPicker puts your table, takeout, phone and delivery orders on 1 screen. Staff check each item as it is prepared, and the order is ready when the last one is checked.

The problem

The ticket says 3 items, the bag holds 2

  • Orders arrive on several screens and paper tickets, in no clear order.
  • A missing drink or a forgotten option reaches the customer before anyone notices.
  • The grill and the bar read the same ticket, and each one looks for its own part.
  • Nobody sees which orders are running late until the customer asks.

What it does

1 queue, checked item by item

  • Every order in 1 queue

    The orders that reach HubRise show up as cards, and a beep sounds when a new one arrives. The queue refreshes every 15 seconds, and paused orders wait below it.

  • Checked item by item

    In the picking view, each item has its own line, with its options and notes. A tap marks it prepared, and when every item is checked, the order is ready for collection or for delivery.

  • 1 screen per station

    Assign your menu items to stations, such as the grill or the bar, by category or by product. Each screen shows 1 station's items, or every station's.

  • A countdown on every order

    Each card counts down to the time the order is expected, and turns red when it is late.

  • Accept, reject or pause

    Accept or reject new orders, start their preparation, pause it and continue later. A station set to on demand waits until staff start its items by hand.

  • Your preparation, in figures

    A dashboard shows orders, items prepared, the average preparation time per order and per item, and the average delay against the requested time, with your most prepared and slowest products.

How it works

From the new order to ready in 4 steps

  1. Step 1

    The order arrives

    A beep sounds, and the order shows its service, its table or number, and its countdown.

  2. Step 2

    Accept and start

    Staff accept the order, then start its preparation.

  3. Step 3

    Check each item

    Each line is checked as it is prepared, with its options and notes in view.

  4. Step 4

    Ready

    When the last item is checked, the order is ready to collect or to ship. Each status change is synced with HubRise.

Questions

Questions about OrderPicker

Which orders does OrderPicker show?

Every order that reaches your HubRise account: Order&Pay and OrderPad orders, delivery platform orders, and the orders rung up on your POS when it sends them to HubRise.

Can each station see only its own items?

Yes. You assign items to stations by category or by product, and each screen picks its station. Items with no station show on every station.

Does it work in a shop as well as in a restaurant?

Yes. In a restaurant, OrderPicker is the kitchen display system (KDS). In a shop, it is the screen where staff prepare each order, item by item.

Can I put an order on hold?

Yes. Pause moves it to the paused orders below the queue, and Continue brings it back.

Do I need a HubRise account to use OrderPicker?

Yes. OrderPicker connects to your store's HubRise account, where your POS and your delivery platforms already send their orders. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
