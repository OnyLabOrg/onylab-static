Order&Pay vs TastyCloud

Order&Pay or TastyCloud for ordering at the table?

TastyCloud comes from digital menus and screens. Order&Pay comes from the order: customers order and pay at the table or for takeout, add a round to the same order, and pay into your own Stripe account.

  • Customers never create an account: they scan the QR code, order and pay.
  • Order&Pay is built to load fast on a mid-range phone, even over a weak 4G connection.

Why Order&Pay

What Order&Pay gives your store

  • No account, built for speed

    Customers scan the QR code and order in their phone's browser, with no app to install, no account to create and no password to remember. The page is built to load fast on a mid-range phone, even over a weak 4G connection, and a product opens as soon as it is tapped.

  • 1 menu for every channel

    Order&Pay loads your menu from HubRise, with its options, combos and price variants. Prices match your POS, and items marked out of stock show as out of stock.

  • Paid into your own Stripe account

    Customers pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The money lands in your own Stripe account, and OnyLab takes no fee on your payments. Stripe's own fees apply.

  • A second round on the same order

    At the table, customers add a drink or a dessert to the order they already placed and pay for what they added. Your team sees 1 order, not 3.

  • Pickup times that hold

    Set a preparation time, switch to rush mode to add minutes, or pause new orders. Customers see when their order will be ready.

  • Apps that work together

    Order&Pay orders go to OrderPicker to be prepared, and their customers to Brevo Bridge and SMSMode Bridge for your campaigns. OrderPad takes phone orders on the same menu.

Being fair

Where TastyCloud may suit you better

Order&Pay does 1 thing: ordering and paying from the customer's phone, at the table and for takeout. If one of these matters more to you, TastyCloud is worth a look.

TastyCloud in short

TastyCloud makes digital menus for restaurants and hotels in France: QR code menus, outdoor menu display cases, dynamic screens, Click & Collect and smartphone ordering. Its back office translates the menu into up to 25 languages.

  • You want menu screens and outdoor display cases as well.
  • Your customers need the menu in up to 25 languages.
  • You want delivery set up in the same tool.

Questions

Order&Pay or TastyCloud: your questions

Do Order&Pay and TastyCloud both use my HubRise menu?

Yes. HubRise lists TastyCloud as loading a menu from HubRise and sending orders and customer details, as Order&Pay does. The differences are in payment and at the table: Order&Pay pays you into your own Stripe account and lets customers add to the same order.

Can I switch from TastyCloud to Order&Pay without changing my POS?

Yes, if your POS receives orders from HubRise. Order&Pay connects to the HubRise account of your store, and your POS, your payment terminal and your delivery accounts stay as they are.
