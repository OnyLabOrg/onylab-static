Next Order

Next Order at a glance

Next Order is a cloud-based POS for takeout, delivery and full-service restaurants, mainly in the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand, according to HubRise. It can be installed on any device and includes a branded online ordering website, driver management and table reservations.

Markets
United Kingdom, Australia, New Zealand
Linked to HubRise
Through Next Order support

What the HubRise connection carries

  • Receives orders from HubRise Yes
  • Receives customer details from HubRise Yes
  • Sends its menu to HubRise Yes
  • Loads a menu kept in HubRise No
  • Sends its counter orders to HubRise Yes
  • Sends its customer file to HubRise No

The 5 apps

What each OnyLab app does with Next Order

Take orders

  • Online ordering

    Order&Pay

    Customers order and pay in 1 tap, with no app to install and no account to create. Their orders reach Next Order through HubRise with the customer's details, built from the same menu, next to the branded ordering website the POS already offers.

    • Menu: Next Order sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to Next Order through HubRise.

    See it on the sample store

  • Kitchen display and picking

    OrderPicker

    Next Order sends its counter orders to HubRise, so OrderPicker can show them next to phone, online and delivery platform orders. In a restaurant it is the KDS, in a shop the picking screen, item by item until each order is marked ready.

    • Counter sales: Next Order sends them to HubRise, so they are included.

    See it on the sample store

  • Phone orders

    OrderPad

    Phone orders are built from the menu Next Order pushes to HubRise, with options, comments and delivery or takeout, and the caller's profile comes up from HubRise. The order is then sent to Next Order, so phone sales sit beside your counter orders.

    • Menu: Next Order sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to Next Order through HubRise.

    See it on the sample store

Bring customers back

  • Email and SMS campaigns

    Brevo Bridge

    Next Order sends its counter orders to HubRise, so Brevo Bridge sees them too. It copies customers and orders into your own Brevo account, sorted by visits, average order value and last order, for email and SMS campaigns to new, VIP or inactive customers.

    • Counter sales: Next Order sends them to HubRise, so they are included.

    See it on the sample store

  • SMS and WhatsApp campaigns

    SMSMode Bridge

    SMSMode Bridge copies the same customers and orders, counter orders from Next Order included, into your own smsmode account and sorts them the same way. Those counter sales count in your segments, for SMS and WhatsApp campaigns.

    • Counter sales: Next Order sends them to HubRise, so they are included.

    See it on the sample store

Questions

Questions about Next Order and OnyLab

Do my counter sales reach HubRise from Next Order?

Yes. Next Order sends the orders rung up at its counter to HubRise, and they can then reach the bridges and appear in OrderPicker.

What do I need to connect Next Order to HubRise?

An existing Next Order account and the optional integrations add-on. The connection is made through the Next Order support line.

Do I keep my menu in Next Order?

Yes. Next Order pushes its catalog into HubRise, so OrderPad and Order&Pay sell the same items and prices.

Do I need a HubRise account to connect Next Order?

Yes. The OnyLab apps reach Next Order through your store's HubRise account. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
