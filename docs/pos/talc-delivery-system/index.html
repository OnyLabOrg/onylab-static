Delivery System

Delivery System at a glance

According to HubRise, Delivery System is POS software from T.A.L.C Informatique for quick-service restaurants doing takeout and home delivery. It runs on Windows and is used in France. The T.A.L.C support team connects it, and a Delivery System license is required.

Made by
T.A.L.C Informatique
Markets
France
Linked to HubRise
Through Delivery System support

What the HubRise connection carries

  • Receives orders from HubRise Yes
  • Receives customer details from HubRise Yes
  • Sends its menu to HubRise No
  • Loads a menu kept in HubRise No
  • Sends its counter orders to HubRise Yes
  • Sends its customer file to HubRise Yes

The 5 apps

What each OnyLab app does with Delivery System

Take orders

  • Online ordering

    Order&Pay

    Customers order and pay in 1 tap, with no app to install and no account to create. Delivery System does not send its menu to HubRise, so they see the menu you set up in Catalog Manager with your product codes. Each order reaches Delivery System through HubRise.

    • Menu: set up once in HubRise's Catalog Manager, with the product codes Delivery System uses.
    • Orders: sent to Delivery System through HubRise.

    See it on the sample store

  • Kitchen display and picking

    OrderPicker

    OrderPicker is the kitchen display for your quick-service kitchen. Delivery System sends the orders you ring up to HubRise, so counter orders appear next to phone, online and delivery service orders. Staff go item by item, catch mistakes and mark each order ready.

    • Counter sales: Delivery System sends them to HubRise, so they are included.

    See it on the sample store

  • Phone orders

    OrderPad

    Delivery-first stores live on the phone. OrderPad runs on a tablet or computer beside your Windows POS, brings up the caller's profile from HubRise, and builds the order with options, comments and the service mode. It reaches Delivery System through HubRise. The menu is set up in Catalog Manager, with your Delivery System product codes.

    • Menu: set up once in HubRise's Catalog Manager, with the product codes Delivery System uses.
    • Orders: sent to Delivery System through HubRise.

    See it on the sample store

Bring customers back

  • Email and SMS campaigns

    Brevo Bridge

    Delivery System sends both its orders and its customer file to HubRise. Brevo Bridge therefore copies your counter customers as well as phone and online ones into your own Brevo account, sorted by visits, average order value and last order, with segments such as new, VIP and inactive.

    • Counter sales: Delivery System sends them to HubRise, so they are included.
    • Customer file: Delivery System sends it to HubRise, so it is included.

    See it on the sample store

  • SMS and WhatsApp campaigns

    SMSMode Bridge

    SMSMode Bridge copies the same HubRise customers and orders into your own smsmode account, sorted the same way, for SMS and WhatsApp campaigns. Because Delivery System sends its orders and customers to HubRise, your counter sales are part of it, not only phone and online orders.

    • Counter sales: Delivery System sends them to HubRise, so they are included.
    • Customer file: Delivery System sends it to HubRise, so it is included.

    See it on the sample store

Questions

Questions about Delivery System and OnyLab

Who connects Delivery System to HubRise?

The T.A.L.C support team does it. You need a Delivery System license.

Why is my menu set up in Catalog Manager?

Delivery System does not send its menu to HubRise. Catalog Manager, included with HubRise, holds the menu with your Delivery System product codes, so OrderPad and Order&Pay sell the same items.

Do my counter orders reach OrderPicker and the bridges?

Yes. Delivery System sends the orders and customers you ring up to HubRise, so they can appear in OrderPicker and in Brevo Bridge and SMSMode Bridge.

Do I need a HubRise account to connect Delivery System?

Yes. The OnyLab apps reach Delivery System through your store's HubRise account. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
