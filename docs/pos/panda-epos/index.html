Panda EPOS

Panda EPOS at a glance

Panda EPOS is a POS for restaurants with 1 or several locations, built to manage delivery and takeout orders in 1 place. HubRise lists an online ordering website, mobile apps, a self-ordering kiosk, a Kitchen Display Screen, waiter pads and a driver app.

Markets

Linked to HubRise

What the HubRise connection carries

  • Receives orders from HubRise Yes
  • Receives customer details from HubRise Yes
  • Sends its menu to HubRise Yes
  • Loads a menu kept in HubRise Yes
  • Sends its counter orders to HubRise Yes
  • Sends its customer file to HubRise Yes

The 5 apps

What each OnyLab app does with Panda EPOS

Take orders

  • Table and takeout ordering

    Order&Pay

    Customers order and pay in 1 tap, with no app to install and no account to create. Orders reach Panda EPOS through HubRise with the customer's details, from the same menu, whether your restaurant runs 1 location or several.

    • Menu: Panda EPOS sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to Panda EPOS through HubRise.

    See what Order&Pay does

  • Kitchen display and picking screen

    OrderPicker

    Panda EPOS sends its own orders to HubRise, so OrderPicker can show counter sales next to phone, table, takeout and delivery platform orders. It works as the KDS in a restaurant, item by item, until each order is marked ready.

    • Counter sales: Panda EPOS sends them to HubRise, so they are included.

    See what OrderPicker does

  • Phone orders

    OrderPad

    Set the menu in Panda EPOS and it reaches HubRise, or load a menu kept in HubRise, as you prefer. Phone orders are built from it with options, comments and delivery or takeout, the caller's profile comes up, and the order goes to Panda EPOS.

    • Menu: Panda EPOS sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to Panda EPOS through HubRise.

    See what OrderPad does

Bring customers back

  • Email and SMS campaigns

    Brevo Bridge

    Panda EPOS sends both its orders and its customers to HubRise, so Brevo Bridge sees counter customers as well as phone, table and takeout ones. It copies them into your own Brevo account, sorted by number of orders, total spent and order dates, for email and SMS campaigns.

    • Counter sales: Panda EPOS sends them to HubRise, so they are included.
    • Customer file: Panda EPOS sends it to HubRise, so it is included.

    See what Brevo Bridge does

  • SMS campaigns

    SMSMode Bridge

    SMSMode Bridge works on the same data: customers and orders, counter included, copied into your own smsmode account and sorted by number of orders, total spent and order dates. Use it to reach new, VIP or inactive customers over SMS.

    • Counter sales: Panda EPOS sends them to HubRise, so they are included.
    • Customer file: Panda EPOS sends it to HubRise, so it is included.

    See what SMSMode Bridge does

Questions

Questions about Panda EPOS and OnyLab

Can I manage my menu in Panda EPOS or in HubRise?

Either. Panda EPOS sends its menu to HubRise and can also load a menu kept there.

Do my Panda EPOS counter sales and customers reach HubRise?

Yes. Panda EPOS sends its orders and its customers to HubRise, so the bridges can use them.

Where do I connect Panda EPOS to HubRise?

From the Panda EPOS back office, with an existing Panda EPOS account.

Do I need a HubRise account to connect Panda EPOS?

Yes. The OnyLab apps reach Panda EPOS through your store's HubRise account. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
