AKK EPOS at a glance

The 5 apps

What each OnyLab app does with AKK EPOS

Take orders

  • Table and takeout ordering

    Order&Pay

    Customers order and pay in 1 tap, with no app to install and no account to create. AKK EPOS sends its menu to HubRise, so Order&Pay sells your dishes at your prices, and each order is delivered to AKK EPOS through HubRise.

    • Menu: AKK EPOS sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to AKK EPOS through HubRise.

    See what Order&Pay does

  • Kitchen display and picking screen

    OrderPicker

    OrderPicker is the kitchen display for the orders that reach you through HubRise. Staff go dish by dish, then mark the order ready, which helps with orders full of modifications. Sales rung up on AKK EPOS are not sent to HubRise, so they do not show up.

    • Counter sales: they stay in AKK EPOS. Only the orders that pass through HubRise are included.

    See what OrderPicker does

  • Phone orders

    OrderPad

    Phone orders suit a Thai restaurant with regulars and takeout. Staff take the call on a tablet or a computer, the caller's profile comes up from HubRise, and the order is built from the menu AKK sends to HubRise, with options and comments, then reaches the POS through HubRise.

    • Menu: AKK EPOS sends it to HubRise, so the items and prices match the POS.
    • Orders: sent to AKK EPOS through HubRise.

    See what OrderPad does

Bring customers back

  • Email and SMS campaigns

    Brevo Bridge

    Brevo Bridge copies HubRise customers and orders into your own Brevo account, sorted by visits, average order value and last order, in segments such as new, VIP and inactive. Customer details sent to HubRise reach AKK, but AKK does not send its own customers or counter sales back.

    • Counter sales: they stay in AKK EPOS. Only the orders that pass through HubRise are included.

    See what Brevo Bridge does

  • SMS and WhatsApp campaigns

    SMSMode Bridge

    SMSMode Bridge copies the same customers and orders into your own smsmode account, sorted the same way, for SMS and WhatsApp campaigns. Its audience is built from phone, table, takeout and delivery orders that pass through HubRise, not from sales rung up at the AKK counter.

    • Counter sales: they stay in AKK EPOS. Only the orders that pass through HubRise are included.

    See what SMSMode Bridge does

Questions

Questions about AKK EPOS and OnyLab

Does the AKK EPOS interface stay in English and Thai?

Yes. HubRise says the AKK interface can be switched between English and Thai. The connection to HubRise is set up from the AKK back office.

Do I need an AKK Softtech account to connect?

Yes. You connect from the AKK back office, with an existing AKK Softtech account.

Will my customers' details reach AKK EPOS?

Yes. AKK EPOS receives orders and customer info from HubRise. It does not send its own customer file back.

Do I need a HubRise account to connect AKK EPOS?

Yes. The OnyLab apps reach AKK EPOS through your store's HubRise account. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.
