The problem

Too many screens, and orders typed twice

  • A phone order is written on paper, then typed into the POS.
  • Online orders and each delivery platform come with their own screen.
  • In the rush, a missing drink or a forgotten option reaches the customer before anyone notices.
  • Your customers are all in the orders, but nobody has time to export them for a campaign.

How it fits

5 apps around the menu, orders and customers you already have

Each app reads from or writes to your store's HubRise account, the place where your POS and delivery platforms already send their orders. An order taken on the phone reaches the POS, and the customer who placed it can receive your next campaign.

Questions

Questions store owners ask us

Do I have to change my POS?

No. The apps connect to your POS through HubRise. If your POS is connected to HubRise, the orders taken in OnyLab apps reach it like any other order.

What is HubRise?

HubRise is a hub that passes orders, menus and customers between a store's POS, its delivery platforms and its apps. OnyLab apps connect to your store's HubRise account. HubRise has its own subscription, billed by HubRise.

Can I use just 1 app?

Yes. Each app works on its own and has its own monthly fee. Start with the step that costs your store the most, and add another app later.

Do my customers need to install an app to order at the table or for takeout?

No. Order&Pay lets them order and pay in 1 tap, with no app to install and no account to create.

Where does my customer data go?

It stays in your HubRise account. Brevo Bridge and SMSMode Bridge copy customers and orders into your own Brevo or smsmode account, where you run your campaigns.

Which delivery platforms work with it?

HubRise connects Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat, among others. Their orders reach your POS through HubRise, like the orders taken with OnyLab apps.

Who do I contact if something stops working?

Write to hello@onylab.com. Support is covered 7 days a week, including the weekends when most orders happen.

I already use an OnyLab app. Where do I sign in?

On apps.onylab.com, where each app has its own sign-in.
