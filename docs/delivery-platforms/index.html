Every delivery platform, on the same screen as your other orders

Each platform on this page sends its orders to HubRise. Pick yours to see how OrderPicker puts them in 1 queue with your other orders, and what its connection carries.

Delivery platforms

  • Bolt Food Bolt's food and grocery delivery; menu, stock and delivery details in sync. Many countries in Europe
  • Delicity French platform with a fixed fee per order; menu, stock and delivery time in sync. France, in several major cities
  • Deliveroo Restaurants and convenience stores; menu and stock published from HubRise. 13 countries in Europe, Asia and Oceania
  • foodpanda Delivery Hero group platform; orders and delivery details, menu kept in foodpanda. Several countries in Asia, plus Bulgaria and Romania
  • Glovo Restaurants and convenience stores; orders, customers, menu and stock. 25 countries in Europe, Western Asia and Africa
  • Hop Delivery Home delivery in Geneva; orders and customer details. Geneva, Switzerland
  • Just Eat Every Just Eat Takeaway.com brand; orders, customers, menu and stock. 15 countries in Europe and the Americas
  • Uber Eats Orders without the tablet; menu both ways, stock and the courier's position. More than 30 countries
  • WeDely Luxembourg delivery platform; its menu, stock and delivery details reach HubRise. Luxembourg
  • Wolt Delivery and pickup; menu both ways, stock and final delivery time. Around 30 countries in Europe, Asia and the Middle East

The food ordering platforms listed in HubRise's app directory, checked on November 25, 2026. A platform whose HubRise integration is still in progress, such as Delivery Hero or Talabat, is not listed yet.
