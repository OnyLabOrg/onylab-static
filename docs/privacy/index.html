Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how OnyLab collects, uses, discloses, and protects personal information when you visit onylab.com or use our applications and integrations, together the Services. It follows the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

1. Who this policy covers

For the stores that subscribe to the Services and for visitors to our website, OnyLab decides how personal information is handled, and this policy applies directly.

For the customers of those stores, such as a person placing an order, we process personal information on the store's behalf and under its instructions. The store is responsible for that information, and its own privacy policy applies.

2. What we collect

  • Account and contact details of store owners and staff: name, business name, email address, phone number, and billing details.
  • Order and customer details processed for a store: name, contact details, order history, and the consent to receive messages where the store collects it.
  • Technical details: device and browser type, IP address, logs, and the settings needed to run the Services.
  • The content of the emails and support requests you send us.

On onylab.com, the only thing stored in your browser is your language choice. The website sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Our applications may use cookies and similar technologies that are needed to sign you in, keep your session secure, and remember your settings.

3. How we use it

  • To provide, operate, and support the Services.
  • To process orders and send the messages a store asks us to send.
  • To manage accounts and bill for the Services.
  • To secure the Services, prevent fraud, and fix faults.
  • To improve the Services, using aggregated or de-identified information where possible.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We use personal information only for the purposes identified when it is collected, or for consistent purposes, unless you consent to a new purpose or the law allows it.

4. Consent

We collect personal information with your consent, which may be express or implied depending on how sensitive the information is and what you would reasonably expect. You may withdraw your consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice. Withdrawing it may prevent us from providing some of the Services.

We send commercial emails and text messages only with the consent required by Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation (CASL), and every message includes a way to unsubscribe. When a store uses the Services to message its own customers, the store is responsible for obtaining and recording that consent.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only:

  • With service providers that host, secure, or support the Services, under contracts that require them to protect it and to use it only for our purposes.
  • With the third-party systems a store chooses to connect, such as its point of sale, its payment provider, or its delivery platforms.
  • When the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of OnyLab, our customers, or others.
  • As part of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, under confidentiality obligations.

6. Storage outside Quebec and Canada

Personal information may be stored and processed outside Quebec and outside Canada, including in France and in other countries where our service providers operate. It is then subject to the laws of those countries and may be accessed by their authorities. Before communicating personal information outside Quebec, we assess whether it will be adequately protected, as Quebec law requires.

7. Retention and security

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes it was collected for, or as long as the law requires. When a store closes its account, we delete or anonymize its data within a reasonable time, unless we must keep it longer.

We protect personal information with physical, organizational, and technical safeguards suited to its sensitivity. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

If a confidentiality incident presents a risk of serious harm, we notify the people concerned and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec, as the law requires.

8. Your rights

Subject to the law, you may ask to access the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to withdraw your consent. Under Quebec law, you may also ask to receive it in a structured, commonly used technological format, and ask that it stop being disseminated or be de-indexed.

Send your request in writing to privacy@onylab.com. We answer within 30 days. For information we process on behalf of a store, contact that store first. We will help it respond.

9. Children

The Services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from a child under 14 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. We will post the new version on this page with its new date, and tell account holders about material changes before they take effect.

11. Contact and complaints

Our Privacy Officer is responsible for the protection of personal information at OnyLab and can be reached at privacy@onylab.com.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or, in Quebec, with the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
